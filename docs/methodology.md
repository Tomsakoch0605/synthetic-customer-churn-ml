# Methodology / Metodología

- **Dataset**: entirely synthetic subscription records generated by a fixed random seed. None of the simulated relationships represent real business behavior.
- **Objective**: binary churn classification in a hypothetical monthly subscription service, not employment, credit, or eligibility decisions.
- **Features**: invented tenure, fee, tickets, logins, payment failures, plan type, and auto-renew indicator. Synthetic customer_id is excluded from prediction.
- **Design**: stratified 80/20 holdout; 5-fold stratified CV exclusively within training to select between constant prior baseline and logistic regression.
- **Preprocessing**: imputation, scaling, and one-hot encoding are fitted inside pipeline on each training fold to prevent train-test leakage.
- **Metric selection**: ROC-AUC for model comparison, average precision for class-aware review. Fixed 0.5 classification threshold (not optimized on test).
- **Interpretation**: permutation importance is evaluated on held-out synthetic records. Importance does not establish causality.
- **Constraints**: synthetic data is intentionally easier than live production data and cannot validate real-world performance, business outcomes or fairness. No deployable model or real subscriptions are provided.
- **Security**: serialized `model.joblib` is generated locally and must only be loaded from trusted origins; do not deserialize arbitrary third-party files.
